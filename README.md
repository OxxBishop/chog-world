# Chog World

A living world where your Chog comes alive, learns from you, and becomes your little buddy.

Chog World floats in the sky above a sea of clouds. Downtown sits in the middle with Chog Hub, the café where every Chog can grab a seat. Bridges lead out to neighbourhood islands, where each Chog has its own dome with a garden and a small modern home. The sky follows your real time of day, and at night a purple aurora rolls in.

Chogathon 2026 entry.

## Status

Early build. The world is in: the floating islands, downtown and Chog Hub, three neighbourhoods of domes, bridges, waterfalls, drifting rocks, day and night, and the aurora. Chogs, wallet sign-in and chat come next.

## Run it

```
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open http://localhost:5173.

Controls: drag to look around, WASD to fly, Space to go up, C to go down, Shift for speed, scroll to glide, double-click to fly to a spot. Add `?view=below&hour=22` to the address to jump to a view and a time.
